1  	# 📻 StarPlayrX
2  	
3  	**A lightweight, fully accessible SiriusXM player for Mac, built for people who use VoiceOver.**
4  	
5  	StarPlayrX was built for visually impaired listeners, a group of paying subscribers that SiriusXM has long ignored. It aims for 100% VoiceOver support, a small CPU and memory footprint, and low-latency streaming.

47 	- Lives in the menu bar (📻) with no Dock icon
48 	- **Tear-off window:** drag the panel off the menu bar and it becomes a floating window you can park anywhere. Click the 📻 icon to show or hide it
49 	- **Window shade:** double-click the header to roll the window up to just what's playing and the equalizer, and again to roll it back down. Moves between menu, tear-off and shade are animated
50 	- **Lava-lamp color equalizer:** live bars driven by the app's own audio (Core Audio process tap + FFT), with drifting colors and a glow that pulses on the beat. Needs macOS 14.2 or later
51 	- **Liquid Glass** look on macOS 26 and later
52 	- **AppleScript support:** play, stop, and resume channels, read what's showing and what's actually playing, search, scroll, tear off, park, and shade the window. Open the StarPlayrX dictionary in Script Editor to see every command
53 	- Search channels, artists and songs
54 	- Shows what's playing on the current channel and every channel in the list, with album art that stays put for the whole song
55 	- Xtra channels (1000+), searchable with "xtra" or "music"; tuning in plays the song the list shows
56 	- Play/pause from the keyboard media keys and Control Center, with the album art or channel art showing there too
57 	- Picks up where you left off: the last channel you played starts again when StarPlayrX opens
58 	- US and Canada channel lineups
59 	- Your password is stored only in the macOS Keychain
60 	
61 	**Requirements:** macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon or Intel, and an active SiriusXM subscription.
62 	
63 	**Download:** [StarPlayrX for Mac 1.1.6](https://github.com/AgentiLoop/StarPlayrX/releases/tag/mac-1.1.6) (signed and notarized). All builds are on the [Releases](https://github.com/AgentiLoop/StarPlayrX/releases) page.

77 	## 🛠️ Building
78 	
79 	You need Xcode 16 or later.
80 	
81 	### Mac menu bar app
82 	
83 	**In Xcode:**
84 	1. Open `StarPlayrXMac/StarPlayrXMac.xcodeproj`
85 	2. Wait for Swift Package Manager to resolve packages
86 	3. Pick the `StarPlayrXMac` scheme and press **⌘R**
87 	4. Look for 📻 in the menu bar
88 	
89 	To sign with your own account, change the team under **Signing & Capabilities**, or edit `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM` in `project.yml`.
90 	
91 	**From the command line:**
92 	```sh
93 	cd StarPlayrXMac
94 	xcodebuild -project StarPlayrXMac.xcodeproj -scheme StarPlayrXMac \
95 	  -configuration Release -derivedDataPath build \
96 	  ARCHS="arm64 x86_64" ONLY_ACTIVE_ARCH=NO build
97 	open build/Build/Products/Release/StarPlayrX.app
98 	```
99 	
100	**Regenerating the project (optional):** the Xcode project is generated from `StarPlayrXMac/project.yml` with [XcodeGen](https://github.com/yonaskolb/XcodeGen).
101	```sh
102	brew install xcodegen
103	cd StarPlayrXMac && xcodegen generate
104	```
105	
106	### Catalyst / iOS / iPadOS app
107	
108	Open `SPX.xcworkspace`, choose the `StarPlayrX` scheme and a destination, then build. iOS and iPadOS builds have to be built and installed locally.
109	
110	### Release: Developer ID and notarization
111	
112	```sh
113	APP=build/Build/Products/Release/StarPlayrX.app
114	codesign --force --options runtime --timestamp \
115	  --sign "Developer ID Application: <Your Name> (<TEAMID>)" "$APP"
116	ditto -c -k --keepParent "$APP" StarPlayrX.zip
117	xcrun notarytool submit StarPlayrX.zip --keychain-profile "<profile>" --wait
118	xcrun stapler staple "$APP"
119	ditto -c -k --keepParent "$APP" StarPlayrX-Mac.zip
120	```
121	
122	Re-sign after `xcodebuild`. This turns on the hardened runtime and removes the `get-task-allow` entitlement, which notarization rejects.

124	## 🩺 Troubleshooting
125	
126	**Static, hissing or popping on macOS 26:** while audio is playing, run this in Terminal:
127	```sh
128	sudo killall coreaudiod
129	```
130	More details: [How to fix popping sounds on macOS 26](https://medium.com/tech-blog-today/how-to-fix-popping-sounds-on-macos-26-c2846fc65ad0)
131	
132	**Nothing plays:** check that your SiriusXM subscription is active and that you're online. Streaming needs an internet connection.
