## 🖼️ Album art stays put
- Album art no longer flips back to the channel logo while the same song is playing. When a later update comes back with the same artist and song but no art, the art already showing is kept
- Art is replaced only when there's new art for that song, or when the song changes. The channel logo shows only when a new song has no art at all
- Covers live and Xtra channels, in the channel rows and the Now Playing header
- No flash while new art loads: rows and Now Playing keep the previous art until the new one is ready, and album art keeps its album look the whole time

## 🎧 Smoother background playback
- Improved playback while StarPlayrX is in the background
- Reduced stuttering

## 💻 Requirements
- macOS 13 Ventura or later (equalizer: macOS 14.2 or later)
- Universal: Apple silicon and Intel
- An active SiriusXM subscription

## 🔏 Security
- Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple (ticket stapled)

## 📦 Install
1. Download `StarPlayrX-Mac-1.1.5.dmg` (or the `.zip`)
2. Move `StarPlayrX.app` to Applications and open it
3. Click the 📻 icon in the menu bar
